Compact ancient Han-inspired shallow flared guard, aged warm gold bronze, decorative white-jade curved serpent motif integrated into the guard (a quiet allusion to the white snake, no literal snake wrapped around blade). Slim black lacquer grip with deep red diagonal binding, two fine gold collars, small flattened round bronze pommel with dark vermilion center. Strong red / black / subdued gold identity. Small LEFT inset enlarges guard and hilt, showing actual metal construction and tidy binding, consistent with full weapon.
