Sound effects in this folder come from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0): http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They were trimmed, high-passed, peak-normalized and converted to mono MP3.

  Kenney "Impact Sounds" footstep_carpet_000-004.ogg (heel thump, www.kenney.nl) mixed with
  BenDrain, Freesound "Footsteps_Mud_01" (https://freesound.org/people/BenDrain/sounds/488068/,
  eight single steps cut from it), "Footsteps_Mud_Single_01" (488077) and "_03" (488082):
  the mud layer band-limited 160 Hz-1.8 kHz, -8 dB, +8-16 ms, pitched down 5-15%; the mix
